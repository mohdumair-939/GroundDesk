# Laptop will not turn on, charge or hold its battery

Use this guide when a laptop shows a black screen when you press the power button, says "Plugged in, not charging", the battery drains very quickly, or the laptop shuts down suddenly.

## Laptop will not turn on

1. Connect the original charger directly to a wall socket (not a power strip or monitor) and leave it for 15 minutes; a fully empty battery needs time before it starts.
2. Check the charger light, or the charging light on the laptop.
3. Hold the power button for 15 to 20 seconds, release it, then press it once normally. This resets a laptop that is on but frozen with a black screen.
4. Disconnect docks, USB devices and external monitors, then try again.
5. If the fan or lights come on but the screen stays black, connect an external monitor. If the picture appears there, the built-in screen needs repair.

## Laptop does not charge

1. Try another wall socket and, if you can, another charger of the same type. USB-C laptops need a charger with enough power (usually 65 W or more).
2. Check the cable and plug for damage. Stop using a charger that is cracked, bent or hot to the touch.
3. Clean dust out of the charging port gently with a dry, non-metal tool.
4. "Plugged in, not charging" can be normal: many laptops stop at 80 percent to protect the battery. Check the battery settings in the manufacturer's app.
5. Restart the computer and install firmware updates from the Software Center.

## Battery drains quickly

1. Lower the screen brightness and use the **Best power efficiency** mode in Settings > **System** > **Power & battery**.
2. Check **Battery usage** in the same page to see which app uses the most power.
3. Close apps you do not need, especially video calls and browsers with many tabs.
4. Batteries wear out after a few years. A battery report can show the wear: run `powercfg /batteryreport` in Command Prompt and open the file it creates.

## Stop using the laptop straight away

If the battery is swollen, the case is lifting or bending, the laptop smells of burning, or it gets too hot to touch, shut it down, unplug it, keep it away from anything flammable, and raise a high-priority ticket.

## When to escalate

Escalate if the laptop will not turn on after these steps, does not charge with a working charger, or needs a new battery or charger. Include the laptop asset tag.
