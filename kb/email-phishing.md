# Suspicious email, phishing, or clicked a bad link

Use this guide when you receive an email that looks suspicious, asks for your password or payment, or when you have already clicked a link or opened an attachment you are unsure about.

## Signs of a phishing email

- Urgent pressure: "your account will be closed in 24 hours".
- A sender name that looks right but an email address from an outside or misspelled domain.
- Links whose real address (hover over them) does not match the text.
- Requests for passwords, MFA codes, gift cards, or bank changes.
- Unexpected attachments, especially `.zip`, `.html`, or macro-enabled Office files.

## If you received a suspicious email

1. Do not click links, open attachments, or reply.
2. Use the **Report Phishing** button in Outlook (Home tab). This sends it to the security team and removes it from your inbox.
3. If the button is missing, forward the email as an attachment to `phishing@helpdesk.local`, then delete it.

## If you clicked a link or entered your password

1. Change your password immediately at `https://password.helpdesk.local` from a different, trusted device if possible.
2. Deny any MFA prompts you did not start.
3. Raise a High priority ticket describing what you clicked and when.

## If you opened an attachment

1. Disconnect the computer from the network (turn off Wi-Fi or unplug the cable). Do not shut it down.
2. Raise a High priority ticket straight away and keep the computer as it is for the security team.

The IT team will never ask for your password or MFA code by email, chat or phone.
