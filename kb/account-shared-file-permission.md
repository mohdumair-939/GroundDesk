# No permission to open a shared file, folder or Teams site

Use this guide when a link to a SharePoint, OneDrive or Teams file opens "You need permission to access this item", "Access denied", "This link has expired", or "That account doesn't have access", or you cannot see a team's files.

## Check you are signed in with the right account

1. The error page shows which account you are signed in with. If it is a personal account, click **Sign in with a different account** and use your work account.
2. Open the link in a private browser window and sign in with your work account to rule out a cached account.

## Ask for access

1. On the "You need permission" page, click **Request access** and add a short message. The owner receives an email and can approve it.
2. Or ask the person who sent the link to share it again with your name directly, instead of an "anyone with the link" or "specific people" link meant for others.
3. For a Teams channel's files, ask a team owner to add you to the team. Private and shared channels need to be joined separately.

## Link expired or blocked

1. Sharing links can have an expiry date. Ask the owner to create a new link.
2. Links to people outside the company may be blocked by policy. External guests must accept the invitation email first and sign in with the same email address it was sent to.

## You had access before

1. You may have been removed from a team or group, or the file was moved. Search for the file name in Office.com or ask the owner for the new location.
2. If you recently changed roles, your old group memberships may have been removed on purpose.

## When to escalate

Raise a ticket if the owner has left the company, if nobody can approve access, or if access was approved more than an hour ago and still fails. Include the link and the exact message. IT cannot grant access to a file without the owner's or manager's approval.
