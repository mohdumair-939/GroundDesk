# Account locked out after failed sign-in attempts

Use this guide when you see "Your account has been locked" or "The referenced account is currently locked out", usually after several wrong password attempts.

## Why accounts get locked

After 5 failed sign-in attempts within 15 minutes, the account is locked automatically to protect it. The most common cause is an old password saved on another device (phone email, Wi-Fi, a mapped drive, or a second computer) that keeps trying to sign in after you changed your password.

## Unlock your account

1. Wait 15 minutes. Lockouts clear automatically after 15 minutes.
2. Alternatively, use the self-service portal `https://password.helpdesk.local` and choose **Unlock account**, then complete the verification step.
3. Sign in once with the correct password. Type it carefully and check that Caps Lock is off.

## Stop it happening again

1. On your phone, update or remove the old password in the Mail app and for the `Campus-Secure` Wi-Fi network.
2. On other computers you use, sign out and sign in with the new password.
3. Open Credential Manager (Control Panel > Credential Manager > Windows Credentials) and remove saved entries for internal servers, then sign in again when prompted.
4. Disconnect and reconnect any mapped network drives that were saved with your old password.

## When to escalate

Escalate if your account locks again within minutes of unlocking it even after updating your devices. The service desk can look up which device is sending the wrong password. Include the times of the lockouts in your ticket.
