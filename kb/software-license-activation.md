# Microsoft Office or software licence activation errors

Use this guide when Office shows "Unlicensed Product", "Product Deactivated", a red activation banner, or other software says your licence has expired.

## Microsoft 365 / Office

1. Open any Office app (for example Word) and go to File > **Account**.
2. Check which account is signed in under **User Information**. It must be your work or college account, not a personal Microsoft account.
3. If the wrong account is shown, click **Sign out**, close all Office apps, open Word again, and sign in with your work account.
4. If it still says unlicensed, close all Office apps and restart the computer, then open Word and sign in again.
5. Office licences are checked online at least every 30 days. Connect to the internet if the computer has been offline for a long time.
6. Each user can activate Office on up to 5 devices. Remove old devices at `https://portal.office.com` > My account > **Devices**.

## Licensed specialist software (for example MATLAB, AutoCAD, SPSS)

1. Network-licensed software needs a connection to the licence server. Connect to the campus network or VPN before starting it.
2. If you see "no licences available", all shared seats are in use; try again later or close the program on other computers you are signed in to.
3. Annual licences are renewed every year in July. If the software expired after that date, reinstall it from the Software Center to get the new licence file.

## When to escalate

Escalate if Office shows a licence error with your correct work account signed in, or if specialist software still cannot reach the licence server while you are on the campus network or VPN. Include a screenshot of the error.
