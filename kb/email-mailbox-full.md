# Mailbox full or over storage quota

Use this guide when you receive "Your mailbox is almost full" warnings, cannot send email, or people tell you their messages to you bounce with "mailbox full".

## Quotas

- Standard mailboxes are 50 GB. You get a warning at 49 GB, cannot send at 49.5 GB, and cannot receive at 50 GB.
- Deleted items still count until the Deleted Items folder is emptied.

## Free up space

1. In Outlook, go to File > Tools > **Mailbox Cleanup** > **View Mailbox Size** to see which folders are largest.
2. Sort your Inbox and Sent Items by **Size** and delete or save attachments from the largest messages. Save important attachments to OneDrive first.
3. Empty the **Deleted Items** folder (right-click > Empty Folder).
4. Empty the **Junk Email** folder.
5. Delete old calendar items with attachments if you no longer need them.
6. Wait 15 to 30 minutes for the new size to update on the server.

## Keep it under control

1. Turn on the **Online Archive** if your account has one: older items move there automatically and do not count toward the main quota.
2. Share large files as OneDrive links instead of attachments.
3. Set a retention rule to empty Deleted Items when Outlook closes (File > Options > Advanced).

## When to escalate

Escalate if your mailbox is still reported full after cleanup and waiting 30 minutes, if you need an archive enabled, or if your job requires a larger mailbox (manager approval needed).
