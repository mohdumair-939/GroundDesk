# Printer not printing, offline, or paper jam

Use this guide when documents do not print, the printer shows as "Offline", jobs are stuck in the queue, the printer is not listed, or there is a paper jam.

## Printer shows "Offline" or nothing prints

1. Check the printer's own screen for errors: out of paper, out of toner, paper jam, or open door.
2. Make sure the printer is switched on and its network cable is connected or it is on Wi-Fi.
3. On your computer, open Settings > Bluetooth & devices > **Printers & scanners**, select the printer, and choose **Open print queue**.
4. In the queue, click the **...** menu and untick **Use printer offline** if it is ticked.
5. Cancel all stuck jobs in the queue, then print again.
6. Restart your computer. If you are remote, printing to campus printers needs the VPN.

## Stuck print queue

1. Cancel all documents in the print queue.
2. If they will not cancel, restart the computer; this restarts the Print Spooler service.

## Add a campus printer

1. Open a browser and go to `https://print.helpdesk.local`.
2. Find the printer by building and room, and click **Install**.
3. Alternatively use Settings > Printers & scanners > **Add device**, and pick the printer by its name label (for example `PRN-LIB-2F-01`).

## Paper jam

1. Open the doors shown on the printer's screen.
2. Pull the jammed paper out slowly in the direction the paper travels, with both hands, so it does not tear.
3. Check for torn pieces, close all doors, and let the printer restart.
4. Fan the paper stack before reloading and do not overfill the tray.

## Print quality problems

Faded or streaky prints usually mean low toner. Report the printer label so the toner can be replaced.

## When to escalate

Escalate if the printer shows a hardware error code, if paper keeps jamming in the same place, or if the printer is offline for everyone. Include the printer name label from the front of the printer.
