# Disk full or low disk space on C drive

Use this guide when Windows warns "Low disk space", the C: drive bar in File Explorer is red, updates or installs fail for lack of space, or the computer becomes slow because the disk is almost full.

## Check how much space is used

1. Open File Explorer > **This PC** to see free space on the C: drive.
2. Open Settings > System > **Storage** to see which categories (apps, temporary files, documents) use the most.

## Free up space safely

1. In Settings > System > Storage, open **Temporary files**, tick Temporary files, Recycle Bin, Windows Update Cleanup and Delivery Optimization files, and click **Remove files**.
2. Turn on **Storage Sense** so Windows cleans temporary files automatically.
3. Empty the Recycle Bin.
4. Open your **Downloads** folder, sort by size, and delete installers and files you no longer need.
5. Move large personal files (videos, photos, old projects) to OneDrive or an external drive.
6. In OneDrive settings, turn on **Files On-Demand** and choose **Free up space** on large folders; files stay in the cloud and download only when opened.
7. Uninstall programs you no longer use: Settings > Apps > Installed apps, sort by size.

## Do not delete

Do not delete files from `C:\Windows`, `C:\Program Files`, or hidden system folders, and do not use "registry cleaner" or "disk cleaner" tools from the internet.

## When to escalate

Escalate if the disk fills up again quickly without you saving new files (a program may be writing large log files), or if the computer has a small disk (under 256 GB) that cannot hold the software you need; it may need a larger drive.
