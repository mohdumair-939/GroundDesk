# Reset a forgotten or expired password

Use this guide when you have forgotten your network password, your password has expired, or you see "Your password must be changed before signing in".

## Reset it yourself (self-service)

1. Open the self-service password portal at `https://password.helpdesk.local` from any browser, including your phone.
2. Enter your username and complete the verification step (MFA prompt, text message code, or your registered personal email).
3. Choose a new password that meets the rules below, then confirm it.
4. Sign in to your computer with the new password. If you are remote, connect to the VPN with the new password first.

## Password rules

- At least 12 characters.
- Must contain three of: uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers, symbols.
- Cannot reuse any of your last 5 passwords and cannot contain your name or username.
- Passwords expire every 90 days; you get reminder emails 14 days before.

## After changing your password

1. Update the password on your phone for email and Wi-Fi (`Campus-Secure`), otherwise old saved passwords can lock your account.
2. Sign out and back in on any other computers you use.
3. If Outlook or Teams keeps asking for a password, close them, then open them again and sign in with the new password.

## If self-service does not work

If you never registered for self-service, or the verification step fails, raise a ticket or visit the service desk with your ID card. For security, the service desk cannot reset passwords over email.

## When to escalate

Escalate if the portal reports an error, if the new password is rejected even though it meets the rules, or if you are locked out right after resetting (see the account lockout guide).
