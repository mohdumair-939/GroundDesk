# Keyboard, mouse, headset or Bluetooth device not working

Use this guide when a wireless or USB keyboard, mouse or headset does not respond, a Bluetooth device will not pair or keeps disconnecting, keys type the wrong characters, or a docking station's USB ports stop working.

## USB or wireless receiver devices

1. Unplug the device or its small wireless receiver and plug it into another USB port, directly on the laptop rather than a hub or dock.
2. Replace or charge the batteries. Many wireless mice and keyboards stop working well before the battery is fully empty.
3. Check the device's on/off switch.
4. Move the receiver closer, for example to a front port or a USB extension, away from metal and other wireless devices.
5. Restart the computer.

## Bluetooth devices

1. Make sure Bluetooth is on: Settings > **Bluetooth & devices**.
2. Put the device in pairing mode (usually hold its pairing or power button until a light flashes).
3. If it was paired before, choose **Remove device** first, then **Add device** and pair it again.
4. A device can only connect to one computer or phone at a time. Turn off Bluetooth on your phone if the headset connects to it instead.
5. Stay within a few metres and keep the device charged.
6. In Device Manager, open the Bluetooth adapter's properties and clear **Allow the computer to turn off this device to save power** if it keeps disconnecting.

## Keyboard types the wrong characters

1. Check the keyboard language in the taskbar (for example ENG UK or ENG US). Press **Windows key + Space** to switch.
2. Check **Num Lock** if the number keys do not work, and **Fn Lock** if the top row keys act as media keys.

## Docking station

1. Disconnect the dock's cable from the laptop, wait 10 seconds, and connect it again.
2. Unplug the dock's power for 30 seconds to reset it.
3. Install dock and firmware updates from the Software Center.

## When to escalate

Escalate if the device does not work on another computer either, if a laptop's built-in keyboard or touchpad stops working, or if you need a replacement device. Include the device make and model.
