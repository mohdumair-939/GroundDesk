# Set up or fix work email on a phone

Use this guide when you want work email, calendar and Teams on your iPhone or Android phone, or work email on your phone stopped syncing or keeps asking for a password.

## Set up work email

1. Install **Microsoft Outlook** from the App Store or Google Play. The built-in Mail apps are not supported for work email.
2. Open Outlook, enter your work email address and sign in with your password and MFA.
3. If you are asked to install **Microsoft Authenticator** or **Company Portal**, or to set a phone PIN, follow the steps. Company policy requires them to protect work data. IT cannot see your personal photos, messages or apps.
4. Allow notifications for Outlook so new emails and meeting reminders appear.
5. Install **Microsoft Teams** and the **OneDrive** app the same way if you need them.

## Email stopped syncing

1. Check the phone has internet access.
2. Open Outlook > your profile picture > settings gear, tap your work account and choose **Reset account**. This does not delete any email from the server.
3. Update Outlook and the phone's operating system.
4. If you recently changed your password, sign in again with the new password. Old saved passwords on a phone can lock your account (see the account lockout guide).

## Keeps asking for a password or says "access blocked"

1. Remove the account from Outlook and add it again.
2. If you see "Your device does not meet requirements", open Company Portal and follow the steps it lists, for example updating the phone or setting a screen lock.
3. Personal email accounts in the same app are fine, but sign in to the work account with your work email only.

## Lost or stolen phone

Raise a high-priority ticket straight away. The service desk can remove work data from the phone remotely. Also change your password.

## When to escalate

Escalate if the phone is not supported (very old operating system), if Company Portal enrolment fails, or if email still does not sync after resetting the account. Include the phone model and the exact message.
