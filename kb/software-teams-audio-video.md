# Teams or Zoom microphone, camera, or audio not working

Use this guide when other people cannot hear you in a call, you cannot hear them, your camera shows a black screen, or the meeting app does not detect your headset.

## Check the basics

1. Make sure you are not muted in the meeting, and that the headset's own mute switch is off.
2. Unplug the headset or webcam and plug it into a different USB port. Wait 10 seconds.
3. Close other apps that may be using the camera or microphone (another meeting app, the Camera app, browser tabs with calls).

## Select the right devices in the app

1. **Teams:** click your profile picture > Settings > **Devices**. Choose your headset for Speaker and Microphone, and the right camera. Use **Make a test call**.
2. **Zoom:** Settings > **Audio** and **Video**. Choose the devices and use **Test Speaker** and **Test Mic**.

## Check Windows privacy settings

1. Open Settings > Privacy & security > **Camera** and turn on camera access for apps and desktop apps.
2. Do the same under Privacy & security > **Microphone**.
3. In Settings > System > Sound, check that the correct input and output devices are selected and the input volume is not zero.

## Camera shows a black screen

1. Check for a physical privacy shutter or a camera key (often F8 or F10) on laptops.
2. Restart the computer.
3. Update the meeting app to the latest version.

## Poor call quality

Use a wired connection or sit close to the Wi-Fi access point, turn off your video if the connection is weak, and disconnect the VPN for external meetings if allowed.

## When to escalate

Escalate if the device is not shown in Windows sound or camera settings on any USB port, which suggests faulty hardware or drivers.
